# ScoreWatch – Parking Lot (Future Development)

## Tournament tracking (priority: future)
- Follow teams in separate fall/spring tournaments in addition to WSSL league play.
- Distinguish tournament matches from WSSL league matches; do not merge tournament results into WSSL standings or W-L-T records.
- Support tournament-specific event IDs, brackets, schedules, opponents, and results.
- Allow parents to opt in to alerts per tournament.
- Investigate how GotSport links a team across independent tournament and league events; account for different team IDs.
- Explore 1–2 tournaments per team per season and overlapping schedules.
